# Monochrome launcher marks

Brand marks identify their respective services in CleanFrame. They are not endorsements. White paths are recoloured from the linked SVGs; size and opacity are controlled by the interface. ABC uses a Lissajous mark from the MIT Custom Brand Icons source; SBS uses the feather extracted from its MIT SBS Chill mark; labels disambiguate the streaming services. No fabricated monograms. Utility outlines are original and share a 24-unit grid.

The Simple Icons collection is distributed under CC0, but that does not establish the rights to every individual logo. See `SIMPLE-ICONS-DISCLAIMER.md`, individual source records and any linked brand guidelines. Custom Brand Icons retains its MIT licence; these source records do not grant trademark rights. See `SOURCES.json` for the original revisions and adaptations.

Prime uses the two smile subpaths, not clipped wordmark text. Apple Music uses the note subpath without the outer app tile. These are intentional simplified marks beside full service names. Simple Icons CC0 and Custom Brand Icons MIT texts are included here. The old Australian TV source GPL text is retained for provenance only; no current icon uses that source.

## Extended icon assets

`EXTENDED-SOURCES.json` records the 35 additional SVG assets, their provenance,
adaptations and SHA-256 hashes. `app/app-icons.js` maps public native application
IDs to bundled marks and supplies the Settings > App icons picker. Choices are
stored by native app ID; Automatic restores the catalogue assignment. Unknown
apps use the generic white symbol. Personal TV inventories and app selections
are not part of this catalogue.

The Commons-derived Joyn, Rakuten TV, RTL+, WOW, MagentaTV and Samsung TV Plus
marks are recorded as `PD-textlogo` on their linked file pages. This is the
source's copyright assessment, not a trademark licence or a guarantee for every
jurisdiction. The two MagentaTV files intentionally share the same mark. RTL+
retains the letters and plus, with the coloured background rectangles removed.

`airplay`, `brandtv`, `chili`, `couchplay`, `gallery`, `movies`, `internet`,
`sportworld`, `kids`, `youtubetv` and `unknown` are original generic symbol
alternatives under the project MIT licence, not official service logos.
`cleanframe` traces the F from `app/icon.png` in white and adds a plain square
frame; the original package icon is retained.

Twitch uses the plain mark; SmartTV Twitch uses `twitch-framed`, the same mark
inside an original square outline. They are assigned by native app ID, and each
app's icon can be changed independently in Settings > App icons. The framed
variant is a CleanFrame distinction, not an official SmartTV Twitch logo.

Brand names and marks identify their respective services. Collection licences,
public-domain source labels and the project's MIT licence do not waive brand
owners' rights or imply approval of recolouring or other adaptations.
